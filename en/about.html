Terena

Digital platform for the Terena language and culture

Mission

This platform is part of the Indigenous Languages Platform. It gathers material on the language and culture of the Terena people for those who speak, learn, teach or study the language. It is just starting: the current texts come from published sources, and the dictionary will follow the fieldwork.

Team and partners

The platform is made by the team of the Indigenous Languages Platform, with the Terena community.

Contact

Corrections, requests and contributions: info@plataformasindigenas.org. Any content can be withdrawn at the community's request.

How to cite
